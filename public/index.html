Inside Plimsoll Journal

One journal. Every angle on your trades.

Your numbers, at a glance.

  • Net P/L, win rate, profit factor and max drawdown in rand.
  • Equity curve with its high and low marked.
  • What the numbers say: which pair made money and which one lost it.
Stop rules on every screenToday's loss against your limit, trades taken and losses in a row.

Every day, week and month.

  • Today, this week and this month, compared with the last.
  • Net P/L per day with your green days, best day and worst day.
  • Switch between daily, weekly and monthly bars.

Every trade in one table.

  • Import your broker's CSV or add trades by hand.
  • Search by asset, setup or notes, and filter by result.
  • Mark each trade as followed or broke your rules.
  • Export to CSV and back up to a file you keep.

The story behind each trade.

  • Setup for every trade: pullback, breakout, news and your own.
  • Confidence from one to five, so you can see if it matters.
  • Win or loss and the rand result, side by side.

One entry a day. One lesson.

  • Write about your trading day in a sentence or two.
  • Each entry shows that day's trades, wins, losses and result.
  • Add the lesson, so the pattern is easy to spot on Sunday.
