  • Set your own limits for leverage, risk and daily loss, and see which trades break them.

How BeanByte apps work

Your data stays with you

Both apps keep your trades in your own browser. Nothing is sent to us unless you turn on sync.

Prices you can plan around

Rand prices, a free trial without a card, and a one-time option for people who don't want another subscription.

Your numbers, not advice

The apps show your own figures and check your own rules. They never tell you what to trade.
